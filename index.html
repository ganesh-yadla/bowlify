OUR STANDARDS

How We Keep It Fresh

From certified farms across India to temperature-controlled kitchens.

01

Cold Direct Supply Chain

We work with berry growers in Mahabaleshwar and mango orchards in Ratnagiri. Fruits are transported in refrigerated vehicles within 48 hours of picking.

02

Zero Concentrates & Refined Sugar

Our smoothie purees are 100% whole fruits without added fructose, corn syrup, or fruit purees from concentrate. Natural sweetness only.

03

Prepared Cold to Order

Bowls are blended and fruits are sliced after your order is confirmed. Our kitchen maintains a constant chilled prep zone to prevent oxidation.

04

100% Compostable Packaging

Our bowls are molded from fallen coconut husks and sugarcane bagasse. Certified home-compostable with FSC birchwood spoons.
